ABOUT KELVIN.

Stories rooted in identity.

Born and raised in Washington Heights, New York, Kelvin Thomas is a Dominican actor and writer drawn to authentic stories and compelling characters. His work reflects a connection to the people, places, and experiences that shape who we are.

Whether bringing a character to life on screen or developing stories of his own, Kelvin approaches his craft with a focus on honest performances and meaningful storytelling.
